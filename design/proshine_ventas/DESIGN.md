## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-performance, clinical, and high-tech commercial presence for industrial and domestic cleaning chemical distribution. It merges precision laboratory science with modern digital commerce and operational management.

### Personality & Emotional Response
The interface evokes absolute hygiene, technological authority, structural safety, and gleaming pristine quality. Users should feel assured of industrial-grade reliability, efficiency, and clarity whether they are wholesale facility managers ordering drum inventory or domestic buyers purchasing concentrated surface sanitizers.

### Design Movement
The aesthetic is a synthesis of **Dark Modernism** and **Tactile Glassmorphism**:
- Deep obsidian and charcoal backdrops evoke depth, contrast, and sterile purity.
- Precision metallic gradients (polished chrome, brushed aluminum) lend tactile credibility to hero elements and industrial metrics.
- Vibrant, luminous electric cyan and azure backdrops function as energy points, communicating molecular activity, water purity, and reactive cleaning performance.
- Sharp, crystalline borders, subtle inner glints, and luminous focal states prevent the dark canvas from feeling dull or heavy.

## Colors

The palette directly reflects the chromatic identity of industrial cleanliness and fluid dynamics:

- **Primary (`#0070F3`)**: Deep electric azure representing high-potency chemical formulations, authoritative CTAs, primary navigation triggers, and prominent active states.
- **Secondary (`#00D4FF`)**: Luminous cyan representing active shine, fluid energy, hover glows, and crisp analytical charts.
- **Tertiary (`#38BDF8`)**: Soft aquatic blue for sub-indicators, focus rings, tag pills, and secondary data visualizations.
- **Neutral Core (`#0F172A`)**: Rich, saturated slate-black baseline ensuring deep contrast without the harshness of pure black.

### Surface Hierarchy & Functional Accents
- **Canvas Base**: `#0B0F19` (Deep Obsidian).
- **Surface Elevation 1**: `#111827` (Card containers, table rows).
- **Surface Elevation 2**: `#1E293B` (Floating panels, flyouts, modal dialogs).
- **Chrome / Metal Edge**: `#E2E8F0` and `#94A3B8` used for high-fidelity hairline strokes, subtle metallic linear gradients, and crisp iconography.
- **Signal / Functional**:
  - Hazard & Corrosive Alert: `#F59E0B`
  - Biological / Eco Purity: `#10B981`
  - Critical / Neutralizer Required: `#EF4444`

## Typography

The pairing between **Space Grotesk** and **Plus Jakarta Sans** bridges technical engineering with human-centric transactional workflows:

- **Space Grotesk** serves as the display and structural header anchor. Its mechanical geometry, sharp joints, and tech-forward posture emulate chemical formulation catalogs, technical specification sheets, and bold laboratory signage.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** governs content density, tabular inventory listings, pricing tiers, and administrative forms. It provides warm, frictionless legibility at compact sizes, preventing eye fatigue during lengthy supply configuration workflows.
- Technical meta badges (e.g., pH levels, SDS hazard flags, formula batch codes) utilize `label-sm` with Space Grotesk in uppercase with open letter spacing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a rigorous 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to accommodate both consumer-facing product discovery and dense industrial B2B administrative dashboards.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Mechanics
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4 columns, `margin-mobile` (1rem), `gutter-mobile` (1rem). Product grids collapse into a single or dual-column view. Dashboard tables switch to vertical card-stack representations.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8 columns, `margin` (1.5rem), `gutter` (1.25rem). Navigation docks into a hybrid bottom bar or collapsed vertical sidebar.
- **Desktop (>1024px)**: 12 columns, max-width 1440px centered, `margin` (2rem), `gutter` (1.5rem). Split-pane views (catalogs on the left, filter & dynamic SDS specs on the right; dashboard metrics spanning 3-4 column modular widgets).

The spacing system relies on an 8-point geometric scale. Dense data areas (batch tables, unit conversions) default to `space-xs` and `space-sm`, while e-commerce showcase cards and hero sections breathe with `space-lg` and `space-xl`.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system eschews standard drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Luminance, Precision Ghost Outlines, and Electric Ambient Glows**:

1. **Surface Layering**:
   - **Level 0 (Canvas)**: `#0B0F19`. Flat, non-reflective base.
   - **Level 1 (Cards & Data Grids)**: `#111827` overlaid with a 1px border `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.12)`.
   - **Level 2 (Popovers, Flyouts, Sticky Headers)**: `#1E293B` featuring `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and border `rgba(0, 212, 255, 0.2)`.
   - **Level 3 (Modals, Chemical Detail Overlays)**: `#0F172A` resting on a 60% dimmed backdrop with a localized drop shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`.

2. **Specular & Chemical Glows**:
   - Critical interaction items and primary badges utilize an internal top highlight: `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`.
   - Hovered interactive elements trigger an atmospheric cyan halo: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px -4px rgba(0, 212, 255, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework balances technical rigor with modern refinement through level-2 roundedness (`0.5rem` baseline).

- **Standard Containers & Cards**: `0.5rem` (8px). Delivers sharp, laboratory-grade structure without feeling abrasive.
- **Modals, Hero Panels, and Showcase Banners (`rounded-lg`)**: `1rem` (16px). Creates soft framing for dense photographic and analytical content.
- **Pills, Quantities, and Status Flags (`rounded-full`)**: Complete border radius reserved specifically for micro tags (e.g., "En Stock", "Biodegradable", "Concentrado 1:50") to maintain immediate visual distinction from architectural layout blocks.
- **Divider Hairlines**: 1px subtle gradients fading from `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.2)` into transparent terminals.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Gradient background (`linear-gradient(135deg, #0070F3 0%, #00A6FB 100%)`), text `#FFFFFF`, subtle inner bevel (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,0.25)`), rounded to `0.5rem`. Hover state introduces a luminous glow (`0 0 16px rgba(0, 212, 255, 0.45)`).
- **Secondary (Chrome/Outline)**: Transparent background, 1px border in `#94A3B8` with 40% opacity, text `#E2E8F0`. Hover converts border to `#00D4FF` with a subtle surface wash `rgba(0, 212, 255, 0.06)`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Borderless, text `#94A3B8`, text transitions to `#00D4FF` on hover with active ripple.

### Chips & Badges
- **Sector Pills (Hogar, Hoteles, Escuelas, Empresas, Industria)**: Encapsulated in dark charcoal (`#1E293B`) with a 1px border in `#334155`. When active, borders glow in `#00D4FF` with an icon accentuating the sector in chrome metallic styling.
- **Chemical Hazard / Technical Tags**: Compact sizing (`label-sm`), high-contrast tinting (e.g., pH indicators tinted with soft neon alerts).

### Cards (Product & Telemetry)
- **Product Card**: Surface `#111827`, hairline boundary `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.15)`. Top section features high-resolution bottle/drum packaging with a subtle radial backlight (`rgba(0, 112, 243, 0.08)`). Hover state lifts the card with a slight border shift toward `#00D4FF`.
- **KPI / Metric Cards**: Contain tabular values, Space Grotesk numerals, trend vectors in `#10B981` (growth/savings) or `#38BDF8` (volume dispensed), and fine metallic icons.

### Form Inputs & Controls
- **Text & Select Fields**: Dark inset background (`#0B0F19`), 1px border (`#334155`), text `#F8FAFC`, placeholder `#64748B`. Focused state engages a 1px border `#0070F3` coupled with a 2px outer aura in `rgba(0, 212, 255, 0.25)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Custom square/round targets with 1px chrome border `#64748B`. Checked state renders filled `#0070F3` with a crisp white metallic check indicator.

### Lists & Data Grids
- Clean alternations between `#111827` and `#0E1526`. Row borders utilize ultra-fine 1px `#1E293B`. Active hover highlights entire inventory rows with a low-opacity cyan overlay (`rgba(0, 212, 255, 0.04)`).
